# Data Provenance and Independent Implementation

This document is a technical audit and working plan, not a legal opinion.
**The current RadByte should not be termed a clean-room implementation**: a portion
of existing data is explicitly marked as derived from Minecraft, and the provenance history
of all files and contributions has not been verified.

## What Was Verified Locally

- `Cargo.toml` declares the project license as `MIT OR Apache-2.0`; at the time
  of the audit, `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE` files were not found in the root. The
  declaration alone does not prove that the authors possess rights to all components.
- `RadCore/data/mc777/{blocks,items,entities,menus}.json` specify `vineflower-direct`
  and Minecraft reports as their source in their internal `_provenance`. The exact
  creation chain, revision, and distribution rights are unconfirmed.
- `RadCore/src/packet/configuration_static_tags.rs` contains a header regarding
  automated extraction of vanilla data tags. Do not treat these tables as
  independently created or automatically permissible for distribution.
- The legacy `PROVENANCE.json` and two generators unconditionally named UMPK as the source,
  even though internal JSON metadata describes a different provenance. The labeling has been corrected
  to "local snapshots, provenance unverified". This does not establish who the
  copyright holder is and does not alter the data license.

### Provenance Updates as of 2026-11-15

Re-comparison with [MCCTeam/UMPK, commit a1e743b48d526f5a7645b47ec48c10b65237edf9](https://github.com/MCCTeam/UMPK/tree/a1e743b48d526f5a7645b47ec48c10b65237edf9/data/java/777)
established byte-for-byte identity of local `blocks.json`, `items.json`,
`entities.json`, `menus.json` with the upstream files of the same names. The
SHA-256 hashes stored in `RadCore/data/mc777/PROVENANCE.json` match. This is an
established content match; it does not establish the author of the copy or the direction of transfer.

The [LICENSE for this UMPK commit](https://github.com/MCCTeam/UMPK/blob/a1e743b48d526f5a7645b47ec48c10b65237edf9/LICENSE)
contains MIT and copyright UMPK contributors. At the same time, the JSON files themselves are marked
`extracted`, `vineflower-direct`, Minecraft reports. The presence of MIT in the repository
is not on its own confirmation of rights to original third-party materials;
the question of distributing this data remains open. The generated
`RadCore/src/mc777_data.rs` depends on these four JSON files according to its header
and generator implementation.

The user stated that code was generated by Gemini and ChatGPT, and third-party
borrowings are possible. Original chat logs and specific references have not
yet been provided; the statement itself does not confirm the provenance of each file.

Initial automated comparison of 96 local Rust files against selected
protocol/cryptography/NBT files from Pumpkin, Valence, FerrumC, and Azalea
found no matches of 60 or more consecutive lexical tokens under the
applied heuristic. The comparison covers only a sampling of upstream files,
does not detect all renamings, translations from another language, or rewrites,
and does not prove independent authorship. The status of the remaining code is UNKNOWN.
- The local folder `Paper-26.3/` contains third-party JAR files. `.gitignore` now
  excludes it, as well as `artifacts/` and generated `versions/`. This protects against
  accidental addition of *new* untracked files, but is not a guarantee of archive contents,
  already tracked files, release contents, or Git history. At the time of the audit,
  no Git repository was detected in the provided folder.
- `cargo metadata --locked` returns dependency license declarations, but this
  is not a rights audit: it includes dependencies for different targets/features, whereas
  the actual bundled files, license exceptions, and notices require verification.

## Steps Prior to Open Release

1. Compile an inventory for every source code, data, and resource file: author,
   original source, specific version or commit, method of acquisition, license,
   copying conditions, and evidence. Mark unknown items as `UNKNOWN`, do not guess.
2. Individually verify registries, tags, packet layouts, NBT fixtures, examples,
   logos, screenshots, sounds, worlds, JARs, and decompiled or generated
   content. Determine which of them end up in the source archive and each binary.
3. For dependencies, generate an SBOM for specific target/features from Cargo.lock,
   verify license texts and notices, trace selected `OR` conditions, and
   ensure compliance with their requirements across distributions.
4. Confirm the rights of all contributing authors. Only then add the full texts
   of the chosen project licenses and necessary copyright/third-party notices.
5. Create an allowlist-based verification of release archive contents, including
   recursive inspection of nested archives and generated data. Do not rely
   solely on `.gitignore`; verify Git history and CI artifacts as well.
6. Review current [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
   and the [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula), trademark rules,
   distribution guidelines, and monetization policies. The disclaimer in the
   README does not grant rights to third-party content and does not replace authorizations.
7. For public release or commercial use, submit the inventory, data samples,
   and acquisition methods to legal counsel; the legal outcome depends on
   jurisdiction, contracts, provenance, and distribution method.

## Genuine Independent Process Moving Forward

1. Define the exact scope and first obtain legal evaluation of permissible sources.
   Prepare an isolated environment and an access/contribution log; do not label
   current files as "cleansed" merely following renaming or reformatting.
2. The specification role gathers verified requirements from permitted sources and
   documents provenance without conveying proprietary source code or substantial
   copies of tables. Legal counsel determines what may be used and distributed.
3. A separate implementation team, relying on no impermissible sources,
   creates new code and tests from the verified specification. Role separation
   and logging help document independence, but do not by themselves
   guarantee a legal outcome.
4. For versioned data, consider a legally vetted source with permission or
   local extraction by the end user; evaluate extraction and distribution terms
   separately. Technical download/extraction does not render data legal.
5. Perform comparative checks against a lawfully acquired copy and
   document test fixtures and their rights. Preserve provenance for every
   new file and review results before replacing legacy components.

Do not automatically delete existing tables or the user's server: they are required
for operation and first need a fully verified replacement.
